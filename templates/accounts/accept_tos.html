1. Purpose

Company operates a mock interview service through interviews.508.dev and related websites, applications, communications, scheduling systems, and payment systems (collectively, the “Service”). Company wishes to make certain paid mock interview services available to customers through the Service. Subcontractor wishes to provide such mock interview services as an independent contractor.

2. Services

Subcontractor agrees to provide mock interview services to customers of the Service in the technical languages, frameworks, domains, roles, and areas of expertise that Subcontractor has represented they are qualified to interview in.

For each paid mock interview engagement accepted by Subcontractor, unless otherwise agreed in writing by Company, Subcontractor shall provide the following core service package (“Interview Service”):

  1. A personally prepared interview problem set suitable for the advertised language and field combination;
  2. A 60-minute live video mock interview with the customer;
  3. A 20-minute post-interview debrief session with the customer;
  4. A follow-up email or written report containing interview notes, an overview of feedback, and a curriculum or suggested course of follow-up work for the customer.

Subcontractor agrees to maintain at least one reusable interview problem set for each language and field combination for which Subcontractor advertises interview availability. For example, Subcontractor should maintain separate problem sets for frontend JavaScript, frontend TypeScript, backend Python, backend Go, or any other advertised combination where the language, field, or role materially changes the substance of the interview.

3. No Additional Service Expectations

The parties agree that the Interview Service described above is the core service expected of Subcontractor. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Subcontractor is not required to provide additional tutoring, ongoing mentorship, asynchronous support, code review, resume review, job placement assistance, recruiting services, employment guarantees, or additional follow-up sessions beyond the Interview Service.

Subcontractor may voluntarily provide additional help, but any such additional help shall not create an ongoing obligation unless agreed in writing by Company and Subcontractor.

4. Skill Representation and Professional Standards

Subcontractor represents that they have the requisite skill, experience, and professional judgment to conduct interviews in each language, framework, field, role, or technical area in which they advertise themselves as available.

Subcontractor shall conduct interviews in a professional, respectful, and good-faith manner and shall make reasonable efforts to provide useful, accurate, and appropriately scoped feedback to customers.

Subcontractor shall not advertise, accept, or conduct interviews in areas where they do not have sufficient competence to provide the Interview Service.

5. Availability and Scheduling

Subcontractor agrees to make themselves available during the times they advertise or otherwise make available through the Service.

Subcontractor is responsible for keeping their availability accurate and reasonably up to date. Subcontractor shall not knowingly advertise availability for times they do not reasonably expect to be able to honor.

Company may use third-party scheduling, video conferencing, payment, or communication tools to operate the Service. Subcontractor agrees to use the tools reasonably required by Company to schedule, conduct, track, and complete Interview Services.

6. Rescheduling

Subcontractor agrees to make all reasonable efforts to avoid rescheduling confirmed interviews.

If rescheduling becomes necessary, Subcontractor shall make all reasonable efforts to reschedule with the customer at a mutually workable time.

When possible, Subcontractor shall provide the customer and Company with at least 48 hours’ advance notice of any need to reschedule. If 48 hours’ advance notice is not possible due to illness, emergency, technical failure, or another reasonable cause, Subcontractor shall provide notice as soon as practicable.

Repeated late rescheduling, missed interviews, or failure to communicate with customers may constitute cause for suspension, removal from the Service, or termination of this Agreement.

7. Cancellations, No-Shows, and Late Arrivals

Company may adopt reasonable customer-facing cancellation, refund, no-show, and rescheduling policies for the Service.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  1. If a customer fails to attend a confirmed interview, Subcontractor may still be eligible for payment according to Company’s then-current customer cancellation and no-show policy.
  2. If Subcontractor fails to attend a confirmed interview without reasonable cause or timely notice, Subcontractor shall not be entitled to payment for that interview.
  3. If Subcontractor is materially late and the customer reasonably declines to proceed, Company may treat the interview as not completed.
  4. If the customer and Subcontractor mutually agree to proceed despite a late start, Subcontractor shall make reasonable efforts to provide the full Interview Service.

For each paid Interview Service completed by Subcontractor, Company shall retain 20% of the paid interview rate actually received by Company for that Interview Service. Subcontractor shall be entitled to the remaining 80% of the paid interview rate, subject to this Agreement.

For example, if a customer pays Company $100 for an Interview Service, Company shall retain $20 and Subcontractor shall be entitled to $80, subject to any applicable payment conditions, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, bank fees, or other terms of this Agreement.

Subcontractor is responsible for any bank fees, wire fees, transfer fees, currency conversion fees, payment processor fees charged to Subcontractor, intermediary bank fees, or other transfer-related costs affecting Subcontractor’s receipt of payment.
